Before anything else: what does someone encounter first when they find Research Lab?

C
Recommended Our pick
Unified Front Door
One homepage holds both. Institution-level mission + animating questions at the top. Navigation branches immediately to Programmes or The Radar. Equal weight.

More complex to design well — but the right answer given what you're building.
Why C is our recommendation: You have real content (The Radar), real programmes (MINDSHIFT, COMMUNITEA), and a real institution (Research Lab by SORRYWECAN). Splitting them across two domains or making one secondary undersells both. Berggruen.org and Noema are separate because they serve genuinely different audiences at scale. You're not there yet — one unified home that branches is the right move now.

# SECTION 8: OBJECTION HANDLING

## Common Objections + Responses

### "€99 is expensive for 3 hours"

```
"Fair point. Here's how I think about it:

A typical AI course is €300-500 and gives you videos to watch alone.

This is 3 hours with three practitioners who build with AI daily — not theory, just what actually works.

Plus, you build something during the session that you'll use Monday morning.

If you save 2 hours a week from what you learn, the €99 pays back in a month."
```

### "I can learn this on YouTube"

```
"You can learn anything on YouTube. The question is: will you?

We've all got 47 tabs open. The value here isn't information — it's implementation.

In 3 hours, you'll actually build something. With people who can answer your specific questions.

That's the difference."
```

### "I'm not sure I'm the right fit"

```
"Quick gut check: Do you use AI at work, even casually?

If yes, you'll get value. This isn't a beginner course — it's for practitioners who want to get better.

If you've never touched ChatGPT, this might move too fast. But if you use it and feel like you're leaving potential on the table — that's exactly who this is for."
```

### "Can I get a recording?"

```
"No recordings. Here's why:

We want people who show up and participate. Not people who buy and never watch.

Plus, half the value is the room — the questions, the discussions, the energy.

A recording of that isn't the same."
```

### "I need to check my calendar"

```
"Totally get it. Here's what I'd say:

We're at [X] seats left. Not trying to pressure you, but it will sell out.

If you're interested, I'd grab it now and figure out the calendar later. If you genuinely can't make it, no stress."
```

### "My company should pay for this"

```
"Agreed. Two options:

1. We can invoice your company directly. Just send me the details.
2. Or you pay now and expense it — €99 is usually under the threshold for approval.

Which works better?"
```
